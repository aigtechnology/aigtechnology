About AIG Technology

AIG Technology is an Ahmedabad-based IT hardware business in Isanpur, specializing in quality-tested used and refurbished desktop computers, complete computer systems, and computer spare parts.

With over 11 years of experience in the IT hardware industry, we serve individual customers, businesses, educational institutions, and bulk buyers with dependable computer solutions. We focus on quality-tested hardware, practical pricing, and reliable customer support.

Our goal is to help customers find suitable IT hardware for their business, office, educational institution, or personal computing needs. Product brands, components, and availability depend on current inventory; please enquire about individual listings.
