Privacy Policy

How AIG Technology collects, uses, stores and protects information connected with its website, orders and services.

This notice describes AIG Technology's current website and backend practices. It is intended as clear business information, not a substitute for advice from an Indian privacy or consumer-law professional. We will update it when our data practices or legal obligations change.

1. Who we are

AIG Technology is a computer business based in Isanpur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We sell used, refurbished and new computer systems, accessories and spare parts, and provide technical and warranty-related support.

2. Information we collect

Depending on what you do on the website, we may process:

  • name, mobile number and email address;
  • billing, delivery and service location details;
  • cart, order, invoice, product, warranty and service-request details;
  • payment provider order, transaction and status references; we do not ask for or store card numbers, CVV or UPI PINs;
  • messages, enquiry details, support communications and consent records;
  • for an authorised service request, device model, serial number, issue description and uploaded device images where you choose to provide them;
  • technical information necessary to operate the website, such as browser, device and request information recorded by hosting or security systems.

3. How we collect information

Information is collected when you submit an enquiry, place an order, use checkout, contact us, request warranty or service support, communicate through WhatsApp or email, or use a customer account. Product catalogue data may be synchronised from Firebase, while business order and service records are managed by the backend database. Optional image hosting may be provided through configured storage services.

4. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, confirm availability, create and fulfil orders, coordinate delivery, process and reconcile payments, issue refunds where applicable, verify purchases and warranties, provide technical support, communicate order or service updates, prevent duplicate or abusive requests, maintain accounting and business records, and protect the website.

We do not use checkout information to collect raw payment credentials. Marketing messages, if introduced, will use an appropriate lawful basis and an available opt-out.

5. Payment security and providers

Online payments are handled through Razorpay's hosted checkout when enabled. AIG Technology receives provider references, payment status and related order information so that it can verify and fulfil an order. Razorpay processes payment information under its own terms and privacy notice. Never send card details, CVV, UPI PINs or passwords to AIG Technology by email, enquiry form or WhatsApp.

6. When information is shared

We share only information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose, such as with delivery or logistics partners, payment providers, email or notification providers, hosting and storage providers, technical personnel, accountants or professional advisers, or public authorities where required by law. Service providers are expected to handle information according to their own legal and contractual obligations.

7. Storage, security and retention

Website order and related business records are stored in the PostgreSQL backend configured for AIG Technology. Catalogue and selected website integrations may use Firebase, and product media may use configured image storage. We use access controls, server-side payment verification and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards; no internet service can promise absolute security.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, provide warranty or service support, resolve disputes, maintain accounting and tax records, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by record type. When information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it where reasonably practicable and legally permitted.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and verification of identity, you may ask us to provide access to, correct or update inaccurate information, explain how it is used, or delete information that we are not required to retain. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, although this may affect a service that needs the information. Contact us using the details below; we may need to retain records required for legal, security, warranty or accounting purposes.

9. Children's privacy

Our website is intended for customers and businesses able to enter into transactions under applicable law. We do not knowingly invite children to submit personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted information, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Cookies, storage and external services

The website uses essential browser storage such as session storage for order-access and checkout continuity. We do not currently represent that we use advertising cookies or a separate analytics product unless that product is visibly configured. External links, Razorpay, Firebase, image storage, email and WhatsApp services are governed by their own policies. Review their notices before using those services.

11. Updates and contact

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the website, services, providers or law. The “Last updated” date will change when material updates are published.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@aigtechnology.in or write to AIG Technology, G6, Gayatri Complex, Near Jaymala AMTS Bus Stop, Jaymala Cross Road, Isanpur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 382443, India.
