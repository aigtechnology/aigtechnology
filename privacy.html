Privacy Policy

Effective date and business contact information should be confirmed before publication.

This page describes how information submitted through the AIG TECHNOLOGY website may be handled. It is a general draft, not legal advice. The business should review and complete it before accepting enquiries.

Information you provide

If you submit an enquiry, the website may collect your name, phone number, optional email address, message, and the product reference or name you include. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

How information is used

Enquiry information is intended to be used to respond to your request and provide product-related assistance. The business should confirm its actual purposes, retention period, and contact for privacy requests before launch.

Firebase processing

When configured, enquiry data is stored using Firebase Firestore. Firebase is provided by Google and processes information under its applicable terms and privacy documentation. The website owner must confirm project region, retention, access controls, and applicable disclosures.

Access, correction, and deletion

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, contact the business using its configured public contact details. Add a verified contact address before launch.

Retention and security

Enquiry records should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes and applicable requirements. Firebase security rules limit public access, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

This draft must be updated if the website's data practices change. Confirm the business name, privacy contact, retention policy, and any additional third-party services before publication.
