Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date, retention details, and business contact should be confirmed before publication.

This is a draft privacy notice, not legal advice. The business must review it against its actual data practices, applicable law, hosting locations, and retention schedule before taking live orders.

Information collected

Enquiries may include your name, phone, email, message, and product reference. Checkout collects your name, mobile number, email, delivery address, optional billing address, order items, and payment/order status and references. The site does not ask for or store raw card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, create and fulfil orders, support customers, process refunds, prevent duplicate/abusive requests, and maintain required business records. Confirm actual retention periods and privacy contact before launch.

Service providers

When enabled, product/enquiry data is read or stored with Firebase. Website order, address, inventory, and payment-status records are stored in the business's PostgreSQL backend. Live card/UPI method entry is handled by the authorized payment provider's hosted checkout (Razorpay when configured); the business backend retains provider references and statuses, not raw payment credentials. Hosting providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

Order access and security

Order details are protected by a private, unguessable access token issued when the order is created and held in the current browser session. Keep access to your device and browser private. The business applies access controls, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction, deletion, and retention

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact the business using its verified contact details. Records may be retained as required for order support, accounting, security, and applicable legal obligations. The business must publish its actual retention schedule and privacy contact.

Changes and contact

Update this notice if data practices or payment providers change. Confirm project regions, backend hosting, applicable disclosures, and contact information before publication.
